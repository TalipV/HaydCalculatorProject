## A. Mistakes already made and corrected in this session (check that nothing similar is left)

1. **Quotes used outside their context.** For 13/1/5 the writing session first used two passages
   as support that, read in context, do not support it:
   - FK 1/108-109 "blood that stops differs from blood that continues": the passage is about
     tamyiz (al-Mutawalli's condition 3, alternating black / red), not about intermittent blood
     with purity that crosses 15 days.
   - Ash-Shabramallisi's "يخص ذاك بهذا" (Nihaya 1/340): he explains it as "if it stops at the end of
     the 15 days or within them, everything is hayd"; it says nothing about blood stopping after day 15.
   Both are corrected in open-questions.md. **Check every other place where a quote supports a
   conclusion about a case it does not name.**
2. **Case 18 / 10 red, 10 black, stop** (earlier in the session): the old test expected "no
   tamyiz" without a source; the writing session first did not find the explicit sources and
   hedged. Now: Tuhfa 1/401 and Mughni 1/286 (10 black, 10 red, stop) combined with the order rule
   (Tuhfa 1/402, Nihaya 1/341f., FK 1/102). Check the combination (tamyiz.md, "Order").

## B. Interpretations (no source says it in these words)

1. **FK 1/86 «يَوْمًا وَلَيْلَةً» is a slip for «يَوْمًا بِلَا لَيْلَةٍ»** (1/13/3). Basis: FK 1/108 quotes
   al-Majmu' with "a day without its night" for the same case and gives the reasoning. Alternative:
   the two pages describe different cases. (open-questions.md, question 1)
2. **Ash-Shabramallisi's remark on the mubtada'a is not a contradiction** to the Bughya rule
   (Nihaya 1/340: Ibn Hajar's wording "might imply" a day and a night for the mubtada'a). Treated as
   a KHILAF note, not as a conflicting view. If it is a real counter-view, the mubtada'a cases
   3/14/4, 10/8/12, 3/14/16 lose their "no conflict" status. (rest-of-tuhr.md)
3. **The 15-day window** (rest-of-tuhr.md, top): the Bughya rule is applied only when the first
   blood plus the purity exceed 15 days, taken from the Bughya wording «لوِ اجتمعَ معَ الدمِ زادَ عليها».
   Check that reading of «زادَ عليها».
4. **Classification per hayd cycle** (mustahada.md): that the first cycle stays basic while a later
   cycle is a mustahada case is derived from Tuhfa 1/399 and Bughya (first blood hayd, then the
   mustahada rules for the rest); no source uses the word "cycle" for it.
5. **"Exactly a day and a night" as first blood** (1 red, 14 pure, 20 red): Bughya says «زادَ علىٰ يومٍ وليلةٍ»;
   the test uses exactly 24 h and is marked [IMPLICIT].
6. **"قبل خمسة عشر" means "before exceeding 15"** (Tuhfa 1/399, Ibn Qasim: «قَبْلَ مُجَاوَزَتِهَا»):
   used to treat exactly 15 days as still within the limit (tests with a rest of exactly 15 days).
